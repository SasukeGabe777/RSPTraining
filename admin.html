Learner Paths

A path is a named set of modules that can pull from any hub. Turn on Show on home to give it a card on the portal home screen, Assignable role to assign it to learners on the Learners tab, and Step by step to make its modules unlock in order.

Paths never change XP, tiers, or badges — a module always scores in its own hub, however many paths it sits in.
